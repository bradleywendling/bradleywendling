## Rolespect

[rolespect.com](https://rolespect.com)

A free job search app that tells you when to pass. Six stations and one
Career File: Board, Scout, Decide, Apply, Connect, Prepare. It confirms a
posting is real, scores whether the role earns your evening, and returns a
verdict.

* Free, no account, no app store. Installs as a PWA and works offline
* Your Career File lives in your own browser, not on a server
* It will not invent experience or rewrite your resume for you. That is a
  design decision, not a missing feature
* Built for people with a real career behind them, not for volume applying
